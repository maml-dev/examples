Example 1720

Cron Schedule

Task scheduler timing configuration.

{
  timezone: "UTC"
  backup: "0 2 * * *" # daily at 2am
  cleanup: "0 */6 * * *" # every 6 hours
  report: "0 9 * * 1" # Monday 9am
  healthcheck: "*/5 * * * *" # every 5 min

  rotate_logs: "0 0 1 * *" # first of month
  sync: "30 * * * *" # every hour at :30
}
